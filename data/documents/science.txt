Albert Einstein was a German-born theoretical physicist. He developed the theory of relativity. He received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921 for his explanation of the photoelectric effect.

Isaac Newton was an English mathematician and physicist. He formulated the laws of motion and universal gravitation.

Marie Curie was a Polish-born physicist and chemist. She conducted pioneering research on radioactivity and won Nobel Prizes in Physics and Chemistry.

Penicillin was discovered by Alexander Fleming in 1928. Fleming was a Scottish physician and bacteriologist.
